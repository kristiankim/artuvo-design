# Artuvo Design Business Strategy

## Summary

Artuvo Design is a solo creative studio for solo founders and local small businesses. The first-year strategy is services-first: use client website projects to generate revenue, build proof, sharpen positioning, and identify repeatable needs that can become templates or digital goods.

The realistic Year 1 target is $36,000 gross revenue, with a $20,000 floor and a $50,000 stretch target.

## Target Clients

### Solo Founders

Good-fit solo founders need a polished digital presence before they have a full team. They may be launching a service, consultancy, productized offer, newsletter, course, or small digital business.

Qualifying signals:

- They have an offer but weak online credibility.
- Their current site does not explain what they do clearly.
- They need a fast, professional launch without hiring an agency.
- They care about taste, trust, and conversion.

### Local Small Businesses

Good-fit local businesses are nearby companies with visible website opportunities. The strategy does not limit Artuvo to one local industry at first; instead, prospects are qualified by the business opportunity.

Qualifying signals:

- No website or an outdated website.
- Weak mobile experience.
- Unclear services, hours, pricing, or location information.
- Missing calls to action, booking, quote request, or contact flow.
- Poor local trust signals such as thin testimonials, outdated photos, or no proof of work.
- Inconsistent branding across site, Google profile, and social channels.

## Positioning

Primary positioning:

> Design-led websites for founders and local businesses that need to look credible, earn trust, and turn attention into inquiries.

Supporting message:

Artuvo helps small operators turn scattered digital presence into a clear, polished website that makes people more likely to reach out, book, buy, or trust the business.

## Offers

### Website Refresh

Price: $1,500-$2,500

For businesses with an existing small site that needs clearer messaging, better structure, stronger visuals, improved mobile usability, and sharper conversion paths.

Best for:

- Local businesses with an old but usable site.
- Solo founders who already have basic pages live.
- Prospects who need a practical upgrade, not a full rebuild.

### Starter Website

Price: $2,500-$3,500

For founders or local businesses that need a focused 3-5 page website.

Typical pages:

- Home
- About
- Services or Offer
- Work, Proof, or Testimonials
- Contact or Booking

### Launch Website

Price: $4,500-$6,500

For clients who need strategy, design, development, basic SEO, launch support, and stronger conversion paths.

Includes:

- Website strategy and content structure
- Design direction
- Website build
- Basic on-page SEO
- Analytics setup recommendation
- Launch checklist
- 30-day post-launch support window

### Care Plan

Price: $150-$300/month

For clients who want light ongoing support after launch.

Includes:

- Small content edits
- Monthly analytics or inquiry check
- Seasonal promo/page updates
- Plugin/platform update checks when relevant
- Priority response for small requests

## Go-To-Market Strategy

Artuvo should use two acquisition motions at the same time.

### Social Audience-Building

Primary platform: LinkedIn

Publishing cadence: 3-4 posts per week

Content pillars:

- Website teardown or before/after insight
- Founder/local business website mistake and fix
- Behind-the-scenes build or design decision
- Case study, offer post, or client result

Goal:

Build trust and visibility among founders, operators, and people who can refer website work.

### Local Prospecting

Primary channels:

- Manual website audits
- Direct email or DM outreach
- Local networking and business groups
- Warm referrals

First motion:

Audit 30 nearby businesses in the first 60 days, identify the 10-15 strongest opportunities, and send personalized outreach with one clear observation and one simple next step.

## Revenue Plan

### Months 1-3

Target: $5,000-$7,000 gross

Focus:

- Close 1-2 small projects.
- Build the audit-to-proposal process.
- Publish consistently.
- Capture the first testimonial or case study.

### Months 4-6

Target: $10,000-$12,000 gross

Focus:

- Close 2-3 projects.
- Introduce care plans.
- Document 2 case studies.
- Identify repeated client needs.

### Months 7-9

Target: $9,000-$11,000 gross

Focus:

- Raise prices modestly if close rate and demand support it.
- Package repeated patterns into a template or digital product.
- Keep one client project active at a time.

### Months 10-12

Target: $12,000-$16,000 gross

Focus:

- Stabilize pipeline through referrals, local relationships, content, and care plans.
- Create a small product/template library.
- Review whether Artuvo should remain a side gig or expand.

## Operating Model

Weekly time budget:

- Client work: 5 hours
- Marketing/content: 2 hours
- Sales/admin: 1 hour
- Product/template development: 1-2 hours

Operating rules:

- Run one active client build at a time.
- Keep projects to 4-6 week cycles.
- Use fixed scopes and clear milestones.
- Ask every client for a testimonial, case study permission, and two warm introductions.
- Do not take complex apps, heavy e-commerce, large branding systems, or open-ended maintenance in Year 1 unless priced separately.

## Monthly Review Metrics

Track:

- Leads found
- Audit messages sent
- Replies
- Calls booked
- Proposals sent
- Projects closed
- Average project value
- Gross revenue
- Care plan signups
- Social posts published
- Inbound conversations
- Referrals received
- Product/template revenue

## Validation Milestones

Month 3:

- At least 1 paid client
- 3 qualified sales conversations
- Repeatable audit-to-proposal process

Month 6:

- At least $15,000 cumulative gross revenue
- 2 public case studies
- 1 care plan client

Month 12:

- $36,000 gross revenue
- 3+ public case studies
- Recurring support revenue
- One small product/template library
